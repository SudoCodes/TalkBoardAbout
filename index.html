Our project primarily focuses on augmenting the spaCy NLP pipeline for improved interpretation of complex spoken mathematical expressions. To demonstrate this, we developed TalkBoard, a virtual whiteboard. TalkBoard visualizes speech-to-text mathematical input and performs operations, thereby addressing real-world challenges such as time-consuming and error-prone equation derivations in educational settings. Currently, TalkBoard covers derivatives and indefinite integrals. It integrates a fine-tuned DistilBERT model with our enhanced spaCy pipeline for command understanding and utilizes SymPy for computations.
